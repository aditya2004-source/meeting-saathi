Meeting Saathi settings

The extension runs locally in your Chrome. Your meeting audio is sent directly to your own Google Gemini account for transcription and document generation — that is the only place it goes. There is no Meeting Saathi server: nobody else ever receives or stores your audio, transcript, API key, or documents.

Gemini API key (required)

Get a free key at aistudio.google.com/apikey. Stored only in this browser (chrome.storage.local) — anyone with access to this computer can read it, but no website or other extension can.

Model & processing

Used for transcription + document generation (about 5 requests per short meeting, ~8 with Quality mode). The Gemini free tier is rate-limited — if you hit "quota exceeded", wait a minute and click Resume on the dashboard, or add billing to your key, or switch to a -lite model. Model ids change — see Google's list.
