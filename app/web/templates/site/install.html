Setup

Install the extension

A one-time setup. Once connected, Meeting Saathi records from your own browser tab -- no bot joins your call.

Download Extension (.zip)
1

Extract the ZIP

Keep the extracted folder somewhere permanent -- Chrome loads it from that folder every time it starts.

2

Open Chrome's extensions page

Go to chrome://extensions in your address bar.

3

Turn on Developer mode

Use the toggle in the top-right corner of that page.

4

Load unpacked

Click "Load unpacked" and select the folder you extracted in step 1.

5

Pin it to your toolbar

Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin Meeting Saathi so it's always one click away.

6

Connect your account

Click below once the extension is loaded. This links it to your Meeting Saathi account -- no code to type in.

7

Allow microphone access

Chrome will ask for microphone permission the first time you start recording -- allow it.

You are responsible for informing meeting participants and obtaining any consent or permission required by law before recording. See our AI Disclaimer.
