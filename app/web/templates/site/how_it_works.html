How it works

From your first meeting to finished documents

Five stages, start to finish. No bot joins your call, and nothing is generated you didn't ask for.

{{ icon('account') }}

1. Create your account

Sign up with your name and email. Review and accept the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Refund Policy, and AI Disclaimer, then verify your email with a one-time code. Your 3 free meetings activate immediately -- no card required.

{{ icon('puzzle') }}

2. Install the extension

Download the extension from the install page and load it into Chrome. Connect it to your account with one click -- there's no access code to copy or type in, and the connection is scoped to your account only.

{{ icon('camera') }}

3. Join your Google Meet call as usual

Meeting Saathi doesn't add a separate participant to your call. It records from your own browser tab, so the meeting looks exactly the same to everyone on it.

{{ icon('mic') }}

4. Start recording with one click

A visible recording indicator shows the call is being captured. You're responsible for letting other participants know and getting any consent required in your jurisdiction before recording.

{{ icon('gear') }}

5. AI processing, then your documents

Once the meeting ends, your recording is transcribed and analyzed automatically -- no manual step required. Meeting Saathi identifies speakers, decisions, action items, and any process discussed. Within a few minutes, three documents appear in your dashboard:

Document

Minutes of Meeting

Attendees, discussion points, decisions, and action items.

Document

Meeting Analysis

A deeper read on risks, open questions, and recommendations.

Document

Business Process Flow

A real flowchart of any process walked through on the call.

Every document can be reviewed in your browser or downloaded as a PDF. As with any AI-generated content, review it before relying on or sharing it -- see the AI Disclaimer.
