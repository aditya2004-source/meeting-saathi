# Meeting Analysis

**Meeting:** Client Requirement Discussion — Acme Retail
**Date:** 5 September 2026

## Key Observations
The current process is well understood by the team, but the rejection and
change-request paths are noticeably less defined than the "happy path" for
a request.

## Risks
- The discount-approval bottleneck could delay several requests at once if
  the director is unavailable for an extended period.
- Change requests are not tracked, making it difficult to size the actual
  cost of supporting mid-delivery scope changes.

## Discussion Analysis
Roughly a third of the conversation focused on the approval bottleneck,
suggesting it is the team's biggest pain point today, ahead of the handoff
and communication issues raised later in the call.

## Recommendations
- Confirm the delegate-approval policy and set a rollout date.
- Introduce a lightweight change-request tracking field before deciding on
  a larger process change.
- Add a proactive customer status update during the delivery phase to
  address the communication gap raised in the meeting.
