# Minutes of Meeting

**Meeting:** Client Requirement Discussion — Acme Retail
**Date:** 5 September 2026
**Attendees:** Priya Shah, Rahul Mehta

## Meeting Overview
The team reviewed how new customer requests currently move through the
order-approval process, and discussed where delays and manual work are
concentrated.

## Discussion Highlights
- The intake process relies on a manual CRM check before a request can be
  validated, which is a recurring bottleneck.
- Discount approvals above 15% require director sign-off, and the director's
  availability is often the limiting factor.
- Customers currently only hear from the team at the start and end of a
  request, which has hurt satisfaction scores.

## Decisions
- Approval is required before delivery planning begins on any request.
- A delegate-approver rule will be introduced for discount sign-off when the
  director is unavailable.

## Action Items

- Rahul Mehta — Propose a weekly automated status update for customers in

## Open Questions
