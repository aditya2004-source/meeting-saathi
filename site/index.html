How it works

Three steps. The middle one is the difference.

1

Capture

The extension records your live Meet call in the background, or you upload a recording from anywhere. You get a transcript with speaker labels in a couple of minutes.

2

Review

Check and fix the speaker names and the meeting understanding — topics, decisions, action items, numbers. This is the accuracy you control, before a single document is written.

No other tool puts this step in your hands.

3

Generate

Pick one or more templates. Each document is written from the reviewed understanding, grounded in what was said, then a refine pass completes and cleans it. Export as PDF, Word, Markdown or a share link.
